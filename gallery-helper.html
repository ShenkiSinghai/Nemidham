Gallery helper

Yeh page aapke liye js/data.js mein paste karne wali lines bana deta hai. Isme kuch upload nahi hota, aur yeh site ke menu mein nahi dikhta. Apne laptop ya phone par is page ko kholein.

1. Photo album banayein

  1. GitHub par images/gallery/ mein naya folder banakar usme photo upload karein (jaise images/gallery/diwali-2026/).
  2. Neeche jaankari bharein aur photo chunein.
  3. Niklee hui lines copy karein aur js/data.js mein photos: [ ke turant baad paste karein.

2. Video album banayein

  1. Video YouTube par daalein, aur uska link yahan paste karein. Video isi site par chalega.
  2. Lines copy karke js/data.js mein videos: [ ke turant baad paste karein.
